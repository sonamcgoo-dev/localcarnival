## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of ability, age, gender identity and expression, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behaviors
- **Respect**: Treat all participants with dignity and respect
- **Collaboration**: Work cooperatively and supportively
- **Openness**: Share knowledge and help others succeed
- **Creativity**: Embrace innovation and thinking outside the box
- **Inclusive**: Welcome people from all backgrounds

### Unacceptable Behaviors
- Harassment or discrimination
- Off-topic remarks or off-task comments
- Physical or verbal abuse
- Persistent disrespect
- Disruption of meetings or events

## Enforcement

### Initial Response
- **Level 1**: Warning, counseling session, behavioral contract
- **Level 2**: Temporary suspension from community activities
- **Level 3**: Permanent ban with procedures for appeal

### Appeal Process
- Formal written appeal to Steering Committee
- Evidence review by neutral parties
- Final decision by Steering Committee Chair

## Reporting

### How to Report Issues
1. **Direct Message**: Contact organizers privately
2. **Committee Reporting**: Submit formal complaint through governance portal
3. **Documentation**: Keep records of incidents and communications

### Response Timeline
- **Immediate**: Emergency safety response
- **48 hours**: Initial investigation completed
- **1 week**: Resolution implemented or formal notice provided
- **Ongoing**: Regular check-ins on behavior
