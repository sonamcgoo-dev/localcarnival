# LocalCircus Governance Structure

## Steering Committee Structure

### Membership
- **Core Team**: 3-5 members with technical and creative expertise
- **Community Representatives**: 2 members from user communities
- **Elected Members**: 0-2 members elected annually

### Term Lengths
- **Core Members**: 2-year terms, renewable once
- **Community Representatives**: 1-year terms, renewable
- **Elected Members**: 1-year terms, eligible for reelection

### Selection Process
1. **Core Team**: Founder/designated by OpenHands
2. **Community Representatives**: Nominated by user communities, voted by Steering Committee
3. **Elected Members**: Open election via GitHub discussions and voting

## Decision Making Process

### Lazy Consensus
For minor changes:
- No objections from any Steering Committee member within 48 hours
- Changes implemented without formal voting

### Voting Thresholds
- **LCAs (LocalCircus Artifacts)**: 2/3 majority required
- **Major Changes**: 3/4 majority required
- **Controversial Decisions**: Unanimous agreement preferred

### Escalation Process
1. **Initial**: Steering Committee discussion
2. **Mid-level**: Committer council review
3. **Final**: Full community vote if necessary

## Conflict Resolution

### Initial Mediation
- Direct negotiation between involved parties
- Use of facilitation tools and neutral moderators
- Documentation of resolution attempts

### Committee Review
- Conflict Resolution Subcommittee appointed
- Investigation of root causes
- Proposed solutions and timelines

### Final Arbitration
- Final decision by Steering Committee Chair
- Binding decision with appeal process
- Implementation timeline established

## Contribution Recognition

### Contributor Tiers
**Level 1 - Explorer**: First artifact contribution
**Level 2 - Apprentice**: 2-5 approved contributions  
**Level 3 - Practitioner**: 6-15 approved contributions
**Level 4 - Specialist**: 16+ approved contributions

### Recognition Methods
- **Badges**: Custom LocalCircus badges for achievements
- **Wall of Fame**: Public recognition on community page
- **Merchandise**: Limited edition circus-themed items
- **Reputation Points**: In-system rating system

### Committer Promotion
**Requirements**:
- Minimum 25 approved contributions
- Positive peer reviews (>95% approval rate)
- Active community engagement
- Demonstrated expertise in specific domain

**Process**:
1. **Nomination**: Community nomination or self-nomination
2. **Review**: Technical and social capability assessment
3. **Election**: Vote by existing committers
4. **Promotion**: Formal approval with responsibilities
